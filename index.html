What this is

A 5K through the neighborhood the day after Christmas, run since 2018 because the weather says not to. Friends from high school, cousins, parents, dogs, whoever shows up. Starts and ends at a house. Trophies are real. The perpetual trophy is the Frozen Icicle of Glory.

We run the FC5K because the weather tells us not to. Because our bodies fight it with every cell. Because together we overcome, together we run.

Questions

Do I have to run?

Yes. You may be excused with a valid doctor’s note. Dr. Backas will be on site with a notepad. Be warned: he may prescribe a 5K.

What if it’s really cold?

Then it’s working. 26°F in 2020 was called “PERFECT.” Rain in 2018 was called “RAIN OR SHINE.”

What if it’s warm?

42°F in 2025 and we still ran. Nobody is checking the thermometer at the line.

Is there a hot tub?

Historically. The Greer Family Hot Tub hosted years one and two; the Backas Family Hot Tub took over in 2021. Bring a suit and find out.

Can I just come to the party?

You can. People have. See the first question.

Is it timed?

Someone usually has a watch. The record on paper is 23:30 (2023). Come break it.

Lore

  • 2018 “The first (annual?) Freezing Cold 5K.” 4:30pm start, in the dark, BYOB, flashlights encouraged. It rained. Finished at a hot tub.
  • 2019 Almost moved for Hanukkah. Hot tub “confirmed to be hot.” Kelly skipped the race and came for the party, setting a precedent.
  • 2020 Outdoors, distanced, 26 degrees. Course reversed for fire pit logistics. Discovery: a mask is actually pretty convenient below freezing.
  • 2021 Event duplicated from the year before; the timeline couldn’t be edited, so the Facebook schedule said 10pm. Everyone showed up at 2 anyway.
  • 2022 Year five. The first FC5K shirt: white long sleeve, splat on the chest, ring on the back. “Selling like McDonald’s hotcakes.” Moved to Partiful “to max inclusion.”
  • 2023 “Holy crap we made it to six.” Hot tub fully operational. 23:30 winning time, 39°F, the only year anyone wrote both down.
  • 2024 Gun moved to 4pm. Extra points offered for adding alcohol to the hot chocolate. Pizza after.
  • 2025 The Ocho. Gather at 2, gun at 3, Zach’s to Jack’s to Kayla’s and back. The trophy came in on the CTA. Branded schnapps minis appeared.

The rules

  1. Gun at 3. Sharply.
  2. The course is the course. Reversals require a fire pit.
  3. You do not have to be fast. You do have to be there.
  4. Rain or shine. Snow or shine. Shine or shine.
  5. Bring a change of clothes and a suit if a tub is rumored.
  6. Hot chocolate is provided. What goes in it is between you and your doctor. See question one.
  7. The Icicle goes home with the winner and comes back next year, by any means of transport.
