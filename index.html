Course
Out to Wilder Park, one loop around the park and the college, back the same way. 4.8 km / 3 mi. No St. Charles crossing.
After
Post race refreshment, award ceremony, party. Bring a change of clothes.
  1. Out. Kenmore to Church, left on York, right on Elmwood Terrace, right on Cottage Hill, into Wilder Park.
  2. One lap. Up the east path of Wilder Park to the top corner. Cross Prospect and go west on Alexander along the top of the college. Left on Fairfield, left on Elm Park, and follow Elm Park back to the park.
  3. Back. Same way you came. Finish at the splat.

You don’t have to run the 5K to enjoy the FC5K. Come late, come for five minutes, come whenever your work schedule permits.

Do I have to run?

Yes. You may be excused with a valid doctors note. Dr. Backas will be on site with a notepad. Be warned. He may prescribe a 5K.

Why

For anyone who doesn’t want to come or run because it’s cold or you’re tired – that’s the point.

We run the FC5K because the weather tells us not to. Because our bodies fight it with every cell. Because together we overcome, together we run.
